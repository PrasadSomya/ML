1. Problem Definition & Background¶

Problem Definition:¶

AllLife Bank wants to increase its loan portfolio by converting liability customers (deposit-only) into personal loan customers. We are tasked with:

  1. Predicting whether a liability customer will buy a personal loan.
  2. Identifying significant customer attributes that drive loan purchases.
  3. Recommending the right customer segment to target for campaigns.

Dataset:¶

  • Rows: 5000+ customers
  • Features: Demographics, accounts, spending behavior
  • Target: Personal_Loan (0 = No, 1 = Yes)

Our goal is to build a Decision Tree based predictive framework that balances interpretability and accuracy while providing business insights.

5. Model Building – Decision Tree¶

In [61]:
# Features & Target
X = df.drop('Personal_Loan', axis=1)
y = df['Personal_Loan']

# Train-test split
X_train, X_test, y_train, y_test = train_test_split(X, y, test_size=0.3, random_state=42, stratify=y)

# Base Model
dt_base = DecisionTreeClassifier(random_state=42)
dt_base.fit(X_train, y_train)

# Performance
y_pred = dt_base.predict(X_test)
print(classification_report(y_test, y_pred))
print("ROC AUC:", roc_auc_score(y_test, dt_base.predict_proba(X_test)[:,1]))

# Feature Importance
feat_imp = pd.Series(dt_base.feature_importances_, index=X.columns).sort_values(ascending=False)
feat_imp.plot(kind='bar', figsize=(12,6)); plt.title("Feature Importance"); plt.show()

# Visualize tree
plt.figure(figsize=(18,8))
plot_tree(dt_base, filled=True, feature_names=X.columns, max_depth=3, class_names=["No Loan","Loan"])
plt.show()

Post-Pruning (Cost Complexity Pruning)¶

In [63]:
path = dt_base.cost_complexity_pruning_path(X_train, y_train)
ccp_alphas = path.ccp_alphas

auc_scores = []
for ccp in ccp_alphas:
    dt = DecisionTreeClassifier(random_state=42, ccp_alpha=ccp)
    dt.fit(X_train, y_train)
    auc_scores.append(roc_auc_score(y_test, dt.predict_proba(X_test)[:,1]))

best_alpha = ccp_alphas[np.argmax(auc_scores)]
print("Best alpha:", best_alpha)

# Final Post-pruned model
dt_post = DecisionTreeClassifier(random_state=42, ccp_alpha=best_alpha)
dt_post.fit(X_train, y_train)
y_pred_post = dt_post.predict(X_test)

print(classification_report(y_test, y_pred_post))
print("ROC AUC (Post-Pruning):", roc_auc_score(y_test, dt_post.predict_proba(X_test)[:,1]))

7. Final Recommendations¶

Final Key Insights:¶

  1. Income and Credit Card Spending (CCAvg) are the strongest predictors of loan acceptance.
    → Target high-income, high-credit card usage customers.

  2. Education: Advanced/Professional customers are more receptive.
    → Campaign focus on professionals and graduates.

  3. CD_Account holders show strong positive loan acceptance.
    → Cross-sell personal loans with CD products.

  4. Family: Families with size 3–4 are slightly more receptive.

Business Recommendations:¶

  • Run personalized campaigns for:

    • High Income + High CCAvg customers.
    • Advanced/Professional education holders.
    • Customers with existing CD accounts.

  • Avoid blanket campaigns – focus efforts where success probability is high for cost-effective marketing.

  • Use Decision Tree rules in CRM systems to flag likely responders.
